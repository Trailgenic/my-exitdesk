# Mike Ye editorial standard

Every resource must help someone do a piece of M&A work. Start with a question, give a manageable first task, say what evidence to request, and explain how the result changes the decision. Define an unfamiliar term where it first appears. Advanced detail belongs after the usable starting point.

## Source-to-guide map

| Saved principle ID | Practical application | Nuance to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| frame-before-detail | Write what is being bought and what could break first | Start before collecting a roomful of documents |
| replicate-the-financials | Follow a sale through the contract, invoice, ledger and cash | Explain timing differences; a mismatch alone is not fraud |
| transferability | Watch someone other than the founder complete the work | Documentation and actual capability are different evidence |
| control-and-alignment | Confirm who can deliver the company and required consents | Scarcity can justify further work only with a credible consent path |
| unknowns-are-findings | Give each unanswered question an owner and required evidence | Missing information is neither a pass nor an invented answer |
| survive-the-downside | Test lower sales and lower margins together, including capital needs | An unsurvivable downside stops advancement |
| buyer-keeps-synergy | Track standalone results separately from buyer-created improvements | Synergy does not increase the seller's standalone value |
| ceiling-and-patience | Keep the private price ceiling visible | Scarcity does not override the ceiling |
| trust-is-earned-in-diligence | Record inconsistent answers and test explanations | Severity, materiality, intent and context matter; no automatic fraud label |
| protect-revenue-integrate-cost | Preserve customer work; move support functions after dependency checks | Six months is an optional standalone reporting window, not a mandatory delay |
| use-contingency-for-alignment | Keep earnout measures, accounting rules and influence explicit | The seller must be able to affect the outcome |
| decision-not-description | Finish with investigate, price, protect or walk | A completed checklist is not approval to close |

Workflow walkthroughs, blank working notes, fictional examples, suggested meeting cadence and the rights inventory are new editorial applications. They are not represented as verbatim Mike answers or a legal determination. Detailed private prompts, task graphs and evaluation sets are not exported into these public-facing drafts.
