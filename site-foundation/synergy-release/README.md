## Model boundaries

Dashboard is the first tab. All figures are USD thousands. The model uses five end-of-year incremental unlevered cash periods, a continuous linear ramp, and upfront implementation cost and capital. It has no terminal value, depreciation shield, or recovery of invested capital. Required costs and dis-synergies remain funded independently of upside probability. Base and Downside are selected explicitly.

Standalone value, buyer-created value, seller sharing, independent affordability, and hard price ceiling remain separate. Missing affordability or ceiling inputs produce `n.a.`. Proposed-price tests do not substitute for decision authority or unresolved evidence. Ten initiative slots and a 60-row realization tracker use stable initiative IDs and relative years 0–5.

The example inputs are labeled Assumption. No Sources tab, source register, macros, external workbook links, or public production notes are included.
