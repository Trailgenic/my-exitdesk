Mike Ye

25 years buying, selling, and restructuring businesses across media, technology, retail, healthcare, and asset management.

Mike Ye is an M&A and corporate development executive who has spent more than two decades leading acquisitions, divestitures, strategic investments, and capital restructuring across media, technology, retail, healthcare, and asset management.

As Vice President of Strategic Planning & Acquisitions at Penske Media Corporation, he led end-to-end execution of enterprise transactions — sourcing, diligence, valuation, structuring, negotiation, and integration — across a portfolio that included Rolling Stone, Billboard, SXSW Festival, the Golden Globes, New Year's Rockin' Eve, The Hollywood Reporter, and Robb Report. He also led the strategic divestitures of BGR and TVLine, and the strategic investment in BuzzAngle Music and Sourcing Journal.

Between L Brands and Penske, he served as Director of Financial Operations at Surgical Care Affiliates, where he oversaw P&L for a portfolio of ambulatory surgical centers and led the controlling-stake acquisition and multi-specialty expansion of Long Beach Outpatient Surgery — an asset later affiliated with SCA and ultimately part of SCA's $2.3B acquisition by Optum / UnitedHealth Group.

Prior to Penske, he served as Director of Corporate Development & Finance at L Brands, where he led the $778M divestiture of Express, supported the $628M acquisition of La Senza, and helped execute a $2.5B capital restructuring. At Intel Capital, he managed the portfolio exit program during a period when the fund monetized $5B+ across 500+ companies.

He holds an MBA in Strategic Finance from Cornell University's Johnson Graduate School of Management and a BA in Economics from UCLA. He is fluent in Mandarin.

He is now the founder of exmxc.ai, an intelligence institution focused on how AI systems reshape institutional positioning, capital allocation, and competitive strategy — and TrailGenic, an AI-native longevity methodology platform.
