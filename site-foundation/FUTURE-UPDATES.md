Create a comprehensive, practical checklist spanning strategy, target development, initial evaluation, valuation, outreach, confidentiality, diligence, approval, structuring, negotiation, financing, signing, closing, integration planning, and post-close review.

Development process:

1. Research credible current deal-process sources and compare them with the existing Mike Ye doctrine and transaction materials.
2. Draft the workflow around real decisions, owners, dependencies, handoffs, outputs, and common failure points.
3. Modify the draft using Mike's documented judgment and language.
4. Submit the complete draft to Mike for review and experiential corrections.
5. Revise and obtain Mike's approval before treating it as canonical or publishing it.

The checklist must emphasize how the company actually operates: workflows, decision rights, exceptions, key-person dependencies, evidence of performance, transferability after a change of control, and rights relevant to data, AI, and workflow licensing.

## 3. Comprehensive post-deal integration checklist — published
