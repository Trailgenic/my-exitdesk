Podcast · Narrated by Mike Ye

The Mike Ye Briefing

A short-form podcast on M&A, buyer behavior, founder readiness, and AI-era insight — helping owners and operators see clearly before consequence arrives.

The Mike Ye Briefing is a podcast about preparation, judgment, and the decisions that matter before the market, buyer, or moment forces the issue.

After more than 25 years across acquisitions, divestitures, strategic investments, restructuring, media, healthcare, retail, technology, and asset management, I have learned that the most important work usually happens before the obvious event arrives. Before the buyer appears. Before diligence begins. Before a founder becomes tired. Before someone else defines your options for you.

Each episode is built around one idea at a time: buyer behavior, transferability, dependency versus leverage, timing asymmetry, signal versus narrative, scarcity versus growth, and what makes a business stronger before consequence arrives.

Season 1 · Six published episodes

Season 1 explores the pattern behind founder readiness and buyer-lens judgment: the owners who prepare early usually have the most room to choose when the buyer, market, or moment finally shows up.

  • Season 1 · Episode 6
    The Window Opens Before It Is Obvious
    August 26, 2026
    Timing asymmetry is not about predicting the future perfectly. Through the Express divestiture, Intel Capital's monetization of more than $5 billion before the dot-com bubble fully burst, and Penske Media's early relationship with South by Southwest following the Covid shutdown, this episode explores why the real question is not only whether now is a good time — but what happens to your options if you wait.
  • Season 1 · Episode 5
    Dependency Often Looks Like Growth
    August 26, 2026
    Growth can look healthy while control quietly moves somewhere else. Through AI search and digital publishing, Long Beach Surgical, Sourcing Journal, La Senza, Victoria's Secret, Express, and cash-flow businesses, this episode asks who really controls the customer, distribution, economics, relationships, and assumptions underneath growth.
  • Season 1 · Episode 4
    The Side They Never Sat At
    August 19, 2026
    Founders spend years looking at their company from inside the business. Buyers evaluate it from the other side of the table. Through Sourcing Journal, Long Beach Surgical, SheMedia, and BuzzAngle, this episode explores founder dependency, transition risk, incentive alignment, and why the buyer lens can reveal both weaknesses and hidden value before ownership changes.
  • Season 1 · Episode 3
    Judgment Is the Product
    August 15, 2026
    Judgment is not prediction, information, or commentary. Through the acquisition of SheMedia, the recognition of Samantha Skey as its hidden leadership asset, and the evolution of South by Southwest, this episode explores how disciplined judgment identifies the people, values, and possibilities that numbers alone cannot reveal.
  • Season 1 · Episode 2
    The Question Beneath the Question
    August 15, 2026
    When owners ask what their business is worth, valuation may not be the question they are really asking. Through a recipe site losing traffic to AI search, a laundromat owner preparing to retire, and the decisions that shaped Rolling Stone, this episode explores the deeper questions of readiness, identity, regret, and what happens after an exit.
  • Season 1 · Episode 1
    Before Consequence Arrives
    June 19, 2026
    Preparation does not start when the buyer appears, the market shifts, or the founder gets tired. It starts earlier. A personal opening on family, health, judgment, readiness, and why a business must be seen the way a buyer sees it before that view has power over you.
