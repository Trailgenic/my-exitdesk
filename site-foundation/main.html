03 / A signature lens · Framework in development

The numbers are the output.
Understand the workflows.

A buyer acquires the people, decisions, systems, and relationships that repeatedly produce the financial results.

Diligence

Test transferability.

Follow one customer order from promise to payment. Ask who does the work, what happens when it goes wrong, and whether someone else can take over.

Integration

Make continuity explicit.

Protect the work that keeps customers buying. For each change, name the person responsible, test it, and agree what would make the team pause.

AI readiness

Separate knowledge from rights.

Record whose data, tools, and know-how the work depends on. Check what can be shared before treating a documented workflow as something the business can license.
