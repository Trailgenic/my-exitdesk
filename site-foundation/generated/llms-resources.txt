# Mike Ye — Selected M&A Resources

> Published resource inventory for Mike Ye’s practical M&A library.
> Entries refer to published resources; planned topic pages and unlaunched products are excluded.

- [Business valuation scenarios](https://www.mikeye.com/exit/valuation): Explore the existing Exit Desk calculator and its indicative positioning scenarios.
- [The five decision frames](https://www.mikeye.com/intelligence): Judgment, dependency, timing, signal, and scarcity: the frames behind consequential decisions.
- [BuzzAngle Music](https://www.mikeye.com/exit/articles/buzzangle-case-study): A strategic investment in music data, viewed through competitive position and capability.
- [Sourcing Journal](https://www.mikeye.com/exit/articles/sourcing-journal-case-study): Founder clarity, partner alignment, and the timing of a capability acquisition.
- [Long Beach Outpatient Surgery](https://www.mikeye.com/exit/articles/long-beach-surgical-case-study): A controlling investment and the operating work behind a multi-specialty expansion.
- [Exit readiness checklist](https://www.mikeye.com/exit/articles/exit-readiness-checklist): An existing seller-preparation checklist, organized around what buyers will examine.
- [The M&A Deal Workflow: From Mandate to Value](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/ma-deal-workflow-checklist): A 156-action, 13-gate buyer-side checklist from acquisition mandate through post-close review.
- [Before You Buy: Follow the Work](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/before-you-buy-follow-the-work): A 112-item M&A due diligence checklist and practical guide for testing earnings quality, workflows, dependencies, transferability, and deal consequences.
- [After the Deal: Keep the Business Working](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/after-the-deal-keep-the-business-working): A 104-action post-deal integration checklist and practical guide for protecting revenue, maintaining Day 1 continuity, and realizing synergies.
- [Gap: valuing a retail business](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/gap-retail-valuation-model): Start with what customers buy, what the retailer keeps, and how much cash sits in inventory.
- [Salesforce: valuing a software business](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/salesforce-software-valuation-model): Connect recurring revenue to the cost of keeping customers, delivering the service, and funding growth.
- [Ziff Davis: valuing a digital media business](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/ziff-davis-media-valuation-model): Look past the audience headline to repeatable revenue, platform dependence, and the businesses actually being valued.
- [Surgery Partners: valuing a healthcare-services business](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/surgery-partners-healthcare-valuation-model): Follow cases, reimbursement, and facility ownership through to the cash the parent company can actually keep.
- [NVIDIA: valuing an AI infrastructure business](https://www.mikeye.com/ma-resources/nvidia-ai-infrastructure-valuation-model): Connect demand for computing to product economics, supply commitments, and the cash that growth can produce.
