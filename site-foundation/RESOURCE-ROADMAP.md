# MikeYe.com resource roadmap

Updated September 10, 2026. The transaction-path architecture and Synergy Underwriting & Value Bridge are published. LOI Economics & Risk Allocator is published. The next major build is Capital IQ Comps Workbench. Build one major resource at a time. The architecture below preserves Mike’s locked specification.


Use this locked resource architecture:

### 1. Corporate development

**Acquisition Mandate & Target Screen**

Decision:

> Why buy instead of build, partner, or do nothing—and which targets satisfy the mandate?

Include:

- Make, buy, partner, or do-nothing comparison
- Capability and market gaps
- Strategic guardrails
- Target-screening criteria
- Transactability
- Evidence quality
- Executive sponsor and decision authority
- Ranked target shortlist
- Pass rationale

This should complement Acquisition Lens rather than duplicate it.

### 2. Valuation

**Valuation Triangulation Tool**

Decision:

> What is the defensible valuation range, and why do the methods disagree?

Include:

- DCF
- Trading-company multiples
- Precedent transactions
- Sum-of-the-parts where relevant
- Acquisition returns
- Standalone value
- Buyer affordability
- Enterprise-to-equity bridge
- Downside case
- Assumption reconciliation
- Valuation range and confidence

Use the five published models as worked examples.

### 3. Financial modeling

**Build the Deal Model**

Decision:

> How do operating assumptions flow into cash, valuation, financing, and returns?

Create a blank, guided Excel model with:

- Historical financials
- Normalization adjustments
- Operating forecast
- Three-statement relationships where appropriate
- DCF
- Comparable valuation
- Acquisition financing
- Sources and uses
- Returns
- Sensitivities
- Checks
- Plain-language instructions

### 4. Industry and transaction comps

**Capital IQ Comps Workbench**

Decision:

> Which companies and transactions are genuinely comparable, and which should be excluded?

Allow an authorized Capital IQ export to be pasted or imported.

Include:

- Company and transaction normalization
- Business-model comparison
- Revenue mix
- Size
- Growth
- Margin
- Geography
- Customer and platform exposure
- Relevant multiples
- Quartiles
- Outliers
- Inclusion or exclusion rationale
- Implied valuation range

Do not publicly redistribute Capital IQ’s licensed data. Publish the methodology, template, field definitions, and analytical logic.

### 5. Deal structure and negotiation

**LOI Economics & Risk Allocator**

Decision:

> How does the proposed structure change economics, control, and risk?

Include:

- Cash
- Stock
- Seller rollover
- Earnout
- Escrow or holdback
- Working-capital adjustment
- Assumed debt
- Contingent consideration
- Financing conditions
- Exclusivity
- Approval and consent conditions
- Buyer and seller risk allocation
- Effective purchase price
- Downside exposure

This is an economics and decision tool—not a legal-document generator.

### 6. Due diligence

Keep **Before You Buy: Follow the Work** as the flagship resource.

Connect it more explicitly to:

- Thesis conditions
- Evidence requests
- Workflow testing
- Transferability
- Key-person and platform dependencies
- Issue classification
- Valuation consequences
- Structure consequences
- Integration handoff

### 7. Synergies

**Synergy Underwriting & Value Bridge**

Decision:

> What value can the buyer create, what will it cost, and how much—if any—should be reflected in the purchase price?

Published September 10, 2026. Preserve the existing workbook and methodology.

Include:

- Revenue synergies
- Cost synergies
- Dis-synergies
- One-time implementation costs
- Timing
- Probability
- Tax effects
- Capital requirements
- Responsible operating owner
- Required integration action
- Workflow dependency
- Revenue disruption risk
- Gross synergy
- Risk-adjusted synergy
- Net present value
- Standalone value
- Buyer-created value
- Amount shared with the seller
- Maximum supported purchase price
- Downside case
- Realization tracker

Mike’s governing rule:

> The buyer may consider synergies when deciding affordability. That does not mean the seller automatically owns them.

Build this as a polished Excel workbook with a Dashboard first tab and an accompanying crawlable HTML methodology page.

### 8. Integration

Keep **After the Deal: Keep the Business Working** as the flagship resource.

Preserve Mike’s rule:

> Protect revenue-producing workflows while integrating cost.

Connect diligence findings directly to:

- Preserve
- Protect
- Standardize
- Combine
- Retire
- Day 1
- First 100 Days
- Synergy ownership
- Risk and decision logs

### 9. Divestitures and carve-outs

**Carve-Out Perimeter & TSA Planner**

Decision:

> What is being sold, what remains entangled, and how will the business operate independently?

Include:

- Legal-entity perimeter
- People
- Contracts
- Customers
- Vendors
- Systems
- Data
- IP
- Facilities
- Shared services
- Critical workflows
- Standalone costs
- Stranded costs
- Separation costs
- TSA services
- TSA pricing
- TSA owner
- Exit milestone
- Day 1 readiness
- Separation risks

### 10. Strategic finance

**Capital Allocation & Deal Affordability Tool**

Decision:

> Is the acquisition the best use of capital, and what is the buyer’s hard price ceiling?

Include:

- Buy, build, partner, or do nothing
- Available capital
- Financing alternatives
- Leverage
- Interest coverage
- IRR
- ROIC
- Payback
- Dilution where applicable
- Downside survivability
- Required follow-on investment
- Opportunity cost
- Buyer-specific synergies
- Maximum affordable price
- Hard walk-away price
