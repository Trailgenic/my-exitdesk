Exit Desk · Partner Program

Partner With the Resource Your Clients Need 1–5 Years Before They Sell.

For business brokers, M&A advisors, CPAs, estate-planning attorneys, and wealth advisors who serve business-owner clients across all revenue scales — from sub-$1M Main Street operators to $25M+ lower-middle-market founders.

Your clients will sell eventually. Most are not ready. When the day comes, they will either be prepared — or they will learn in diligence what they should have fixed three years earlier. Exit Desk is built for exactly that window — the 1–5 years before a client engages a broker or starts a formal process.

A flat-fee buyer-lens audit ($199 below $1M annual revenue; $499 at $1M+) is not a competitive threat to your engagement. It is the resource that makes your clients ready for it.

Already on the market?

For a seller whose process has slowed, Exit Desk provides an independent buyer-side diagnostic before the next pricing or positioning decision. You keep the relationship. Exit Desk does not broker transactions or compete for your engagement.

Two-Way Partnership
How the Partnership Works
Referrals flow in both directions — pre-qualified leads when the business is ready, paid placement when it isn't.
You → Exit Desk
When a client is thinking about an eventual sale but not ready to engage, refer them to the Buyer-Lens Audit™. They get a buyer-side read on what to fix; you stay the trusted advisor in their corner. $100 referral fee per converted client.
Exit Desk → You
When the audit identifies a sale-ready seller in your industry or region, we refer them to partner brokers, M&A advisors, or wealth advisors in our network. Pre-qualified, pre-educated, and arriving with a buyer-lens analysis already in hand.
No Conflict
Exit Desk doesn't represent sellers, doesn't compete for engagements, doesn't earn on transaction close. Different product, different point in the journey. Brokers help clients sell; we help clients prepare.
What You Get
For Your Practice
A structured buyer-side resource you can offer clients without building one. You look proactive. The client gets a buyer-lens analysis grounded in 25 years and 75+ transactions of M&A judgment, calibrated to their revenue scale and buyer universe — from Main Street operators under $1M to lower-middle-market founders at $15M+. The fee is a small fraction of even the smallest typical sale.
For Your Client
A confidential-memo-style report identifying exactly where buyer scrutiny will focus first — earnings quality, owner dependency, customer concentration, competitive position, AI exposure. Delivered within 24 hours. The client enters any future process with specificity no broker will give them upfront.
For Brokers Specifically
A pre-engagement filter for prospects who aren't ready yet. Refer them to Exit Desk during the preparation window, and we send them back when the business is ready to go to market. You preserve the relationship without taking the engagement before it's right.
