Inside the assessment

The company and competitive landscape

Business model, customer alternatives, named competitors, differentiation, and the capability actually being acquired.

The industry and what is changing

Demand drivers, consolidation, structural shifts, AI exposure, and the implications for this specific target.

The economics and what transfers

Revenue durability, earnings adjustments, customer concentration, owner dependence, management, financing, and required capital.

The buyer’s next decision

A provisional assessment, ranked diligence priorities, seller questions, and distinct actions to investigate, price, protect, or walk.

Public research is cited and separated from buyer-provided claims. Unknown private financials remain unknown. Valuation and return calculations require supported model inputs; the AI does not invent them.

How it works

Your context. Public evidence. Buyer judgment.

  1. Complete the buyer intake. Share the target, buyer mandate, economics, operating facts, and the questions that matter.
  2. Choose the research scope. Authorize a public company identity for company, competitor, and industry research, or keep the target anonymous and use supplied information only.
  3. Receive the assessment. Ella applies the existing framework, cites the evidence behind findings, and identifies the next commitment and its evidence gate.

The public research stage receives only the public identity you authorize. Private deal details are used in the separate AI assessment. Stored intake and report data are encrypted and retained for 30 days. Report access and PDF delivery are tied to the paid order.
